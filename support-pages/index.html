Canva App

Sundanese

Convert Latin text into Sundanese script (Aksara Sunda Baku) and add it to your Canva design as editable text — and convert back again.

Find it in Canva

Features

  • Two-way conversion: Latin ⇄ Aksara Sunda
  • Choose a Sundanese font from Canva's font library
  • Preset text styles (invitation title, body, caption)
  • Transliteration rules built into the app
  • Runs entirely in your browser — no account, no server
