Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The Aksara Sunda app runs entirely inside your browser, within the Canva editor. It converts the text you type locally.

Data we collect

None. The app has no server, no accounts, no analytics, and no cookies. The text you enter is not sent to us or to any third party.

Data stored

The app does not persist your text. Anything you add to a design is stored by Canva under your own Canva account, subject to Canva's privacy policy.
