How to use the app

  1. Open your design in Canva and launch the Sundanese app.
  2. Choose a direction: Latin → Sunda or Sunda → Latin.
  3. Type your text and press Convert.
  4. Optionally pick a font and a text style.
  5. Press Add to design to place the result as editable text.

Troubleshooting

  • The Sundanese text looks like empty boxes. Choose a Sundanese font (for example Noto Sans Sundanese) via the font picker before adding it to your design.
  • Letters look wrong. Transliteration follows Aksara Sunda Baku rules. Check the in-app "Transliteration rules" section.
